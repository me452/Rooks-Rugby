The 7s Circuit Squad

High-Repetition Contact Competition & Olympic Pathways

Rugby 7s is one of the fastest-growing sports in the nation. Played on a full-sized pitch with 7 players per side, every athlete runs, passes, defends, and attacks in space. There are zero benchwarmers on a Rooks squad.

  • ✓ 4 Texas Tournaments (TOLA Circuit): Compete across regional events (Dallas, Fort Worth, Austin) with 12–16 live competitive match reps.
  • ✓ Track-Tackling Safety Curriculum: Certified safe-falling mechanics (S.A.F.E. rolls), cheek-to-cheek head safety, and strict zero-tolerance enforcement against dangerous play.
  • ✓ The Merit Jersey Model: Athletes keep their custom sublimated training singlet, match shorts, socks, and gear backpack, while competing in club-owned match jerseys to eliminate annual kit bloat.
  • ✓ USA Rugby Sanctioned: Certified World Rugby/USA Rugby coaching with secondary participant medical coverage (CIPP).
Bonus Player Inclusions: Full-Contact High School 7s registration automatically includes 100% free unrestricted entry into our local Greenville Summer Touch Series and weekly Wednesday training blocks.

Year-Round Engine

Wednesday Touch & Performance Labs

Every Wednesday afternoon from 2:00 PM to 4:00 PM, Rooks coaches host open non-contact touch sessions in Greenville. This serves as our active recovery engine and skills incubator, integrating the three pillars of our curriculum:

  • 1 Forge (Speed Geometry): Sprint acceleration, deceleration angles, posture control, and spatial tracking.
  • 2 Flush (Recovery): Neuromuscular recovery, dynamic reciprocal inhibition, and hydration education.
  • 3 Focus (Mental Architecture): Leadership roundtables, character literature, and composure under pressure.

Adult Saturday Touch Rugby

Ready to dust off your boots or experience rugby for the very first time? Starting this January, Greenville RFC is launching weekly Saturday morning Adult Touch Rugby.

This program is built on international Federation of International Touch (FIT) rules—100% non-contact, high-aerobic running, and crisp ball movement. It provides the perfect, low-barrier environment to help brand-new players get acclimated without collision fear and bring former players back into the fray to shake off rust and rebuild pitch fitness.

Zero Tackling or Collisions Fast two-hand touch on the hips; ideal for conditioning, joint longevity, and multi-sport athletes.
The "Third Half" Fellowship Rebuilding the legendary camaraderie and brotherhood of amateur rugby in Hunt County.
